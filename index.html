Privacy Policy

App: Checkers • Developer: Joseph Solomon • Effective: October 2026
Privacy-First Architecture: Checkers is built as a 100% offline-first board game. The app requires no user accounts, no social sign-ins, and no cloud databases. All game statistics, difficulty milestones, and session saves remain strictly on your physical device.

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Joseph Solomon ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") manages information in connection with the mobile application Checkers distributed via the Google Play Store.

By installing and playing Checkers, you acknowledge the terms outlined herein. If you do not agree with these practices, please uninstall the application.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All core gameplay progression and customization data is saved strictly to your device's isolated internal storage via AsyncStorage:

  • Game Preferences: Toggles for audio sound effects, tactile haptic vibrations, selected board themes, and rule options.
  • Match Statistics: Total matches played, won, lost, and drawn across all 10 difficulty levels and all 7 rule variations (English, Russian, Brazilian, International 10×10, Italian, Turkish, and Thai).
  • Active Match Sessions: Move history recorded in standard algebraic notation, board tile arrangements, captured piece tallies, and turn indicators allowing interrupted matches to resume seamlessly.
  • Local Pass & Play Seat Names: Custom player names entered for local multiplayer games (e.g., "Player 1", "Player 2"). These names are kept exclusively on your physical device and are never transmitted to any external server.

You can erase all of this local data at any time by tapping "Reset All Statistics" in the in-game Settings screen, clearing the app's cache in your Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because Checkers operates locally on your device without proprietary backend servers, we do not collect, process, or sell:

  • Personal identifying information (real names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses).
  • Account login credentials or social network profiles.
  • Microphone or voice recordings (the app plays local sound effects only and never listens to audio).
  • Camera, photo gallery, or device contact lists.
  • Precise GPS location data.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Checkers is a free-to-play mobile application supported by third-party advertising via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The application presents three standard ad formats:

  • Banner Ads: Rendered at the bottom of the game arena.
  • Periodic Interstitial Ads: Fullscreen video or static ads served at natural stopping points—specifically once every three (3) completed games when returning to the menu or beginning a rematch. Interstitials never interrupt active gameplay.
  • Voluntary Rewarded Video Ads: Optional ads that users voluntarily choose to watch to earn in-game move utilities (Undos or Hints) once their free match allowance is exhausted.

AdMob Technical Data Processing

Our code configures ad requests to request non-personalized ads (requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true). However, Google AdMob and its certified partners automatically process certain technical telemetry to deliver ads, prevent invalid traffic, and enforce frequency caps:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID).
  • IP address (used to infer broad non-precise geographic region, such as country or city).
  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Ad interaction metrics, completion telemetry, and crash diagnostics.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, consult How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Device Permissions & Hardware Clarity

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used exclusively to request advertisements through Google AdMob and verify rewarded ad completions.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to generate tactile haptic feedback during piece movement, captures, promotions, and invalid move alerts. Can be toggled off anytime in Settings.
  • Microphone Clarification: While underlying audio frameworks (expo-audio) may include manifest tags, Checkers does not record audio, access the microphone, or process voice data.
  • Storage: The app operates strictly within its sandboxed internal container. It does not access, read, or modify public shared storage or personal photo libraries.

6. Push Notifications

Checkers may schedule local on-device notifications for match reminders. All scheduling happens locally via device timers. We do not harvest, store, or transmit remote push notification tokens (FCM / APNs) to any external server.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Checkers is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided device-identifying data to third-party ad networks, please contact us so we can investigate.

8. User Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your locally stored information:

  • Reset In-App: Tap Settings > Reset All Statistics within the game to wipe all match records and progression.
  • Android System Reset: Navigate to Settings > Apps > Checkers > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Ad Personalization Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
